# ADR-0006: Autonomy is earned; critical authority remains owner-gated

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-13

## Decision

Autonomy advances in evidence-based levels from assisted build through autonomous staging and reversible production promotion.

L3 critical actions remain explicitly human-gated. The system may not autonomously grant itself the authority required to change that rule.

Routine owner interaction should be exception-oriented: material risks, constitutional changes and blocked decisions, not ordinary engineering confirmations.

## Consequences

- Day-to-day engineering can become highly autonomous.
- The system can fail experiments safely and roll them back.
- High-risk real-world authority does not increase merely because the AI has become more capable.
- Advancement to broader autonomy requires measurable reliability rather than confidence claims from agents.
